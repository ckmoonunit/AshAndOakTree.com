Privacy

We use what you send us to quote your job and to talk with you about it, and for nothing else.

What gets collected

When you fill out the quote form, the site sends your name, phone, email if you gave one, address, work description, the photos you attached, your best time to reach you, and how you heard about us. The form posts to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards it to our email. Photos are resized in your browser before sending, which also strips location data from them.

What gets done with it

It is used to give you a quote and, if you hire us, to do the work. Nothing is sold, traded, or shared with marketers. Photos may be used as before/after references in conversation with you. They are not posted publicly without your permission.

How long it sticks around

Quote requests pass through FormSubmit and live in our email. If you want yours deleted, ask. Same applies to any photos.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set tracking cookies. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views; it sets no cookies and does not identify or follow you across sites. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees that request.
