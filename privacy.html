Privacy

The short version: we use the info you send to quote your job and talk to you about it. Nothing else.

What gets collected

When you fill out the quote form, the site sends your name, phone, email if you gave one, address, work description, the photos you attached, your best time to reach you, and how you heard about us. The form posts to Formspree, which forwards it to our email.

What gets done with it

It is used to give you a quote and, if you hire us, to do the work. Nothing is sold, traded, or shared with marketers. Photos may be used as before/after references in conversation with you. They are not posted publicly without your permission.

How long it sticks around

Quote requests live in email and in Formspree's archive. If you want yours deleted, ask. Same applies to any photos.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set tracking cookies. Google Fonts is loaded from Google's CDN, which means Google sees the request. That is it.
