How we grow

Care for every tree on your property

Eight ways we look after the trees in Cache Valley yards, orchards, and properties. Every job goes through Ben himself, start to finish. Rooted in doing the work right.

✂

Pruning & Shaping

Structural pruning for young trees, crown cleaning for mature ones, fruit-tree pruning timed to the season. No flush cuts, no topping.

⚔

Tree Removal

When a tree is dead, dying, or genuinely hazardous, we take it down safely. If it can be saved, we will tell you that first.

🌿

Health & Diagnosis

Yellowing leaves, dieback, suspicious bark, borers, fire blight. A walk-through and a written plan, not a sales pitch.

⚡

Storm Damage

Wind, ice, heavy snow. Same-day storm response, careful cleanup, salvage pruning where the tree still has a future.

⚙

Stump Grinding

Grind down below grade so you can replant, lay sod, or just stop tripping over it. Cleanup included.

🌱

Planting & Selection

The right tree in the right spot saves you a decade of trouble. We help you pick species suited to Cache Valley soils, water, and winters.

🔗

Cabling & Bracing

Structural support for mature trees with weak unions or stress cracks. Steel cables and bracing rods done to industry standards, so you can keep the tree.

Creating new landscapes for residential builds and commercial projects, from bare lot to finished planting plan. Ben has also done planning work for American Fork City.

Our roots

Ben Ash. Certified arborist, Cache Valley resident.

Ben grew up around orchards in Northern Utah and has spent the last decade learning how trees actually work, in the field and through the ISA certification. The certification is real, the equipment is sharp, and we do not beat around the bush.

Ash & Oak is a family-run business. Ben handles every job himself, start to finish. Outside residential work, he has done landscape and tree-management planning for American Fork City, designing planting plans and long-term care schedules for municipal trees.

We would rather talk you out of a removal you do not need than charge you for one. That is the whole business.

Most jobs are a single visit. Bigger projects get a written scope before any chainsaw runs. Insurance and credentials available on request, because you should ask.
