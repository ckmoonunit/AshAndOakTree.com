Our values

Cared For

Every property has its own story: how you use the yard, what you want to see from the window, and what you can spend. We start there. The care plan fits your life and budget, and every tree still gets what it needs to stay healthy for decades.

Supported

It all starts at the roots. That means good soil, the right species, and the right spot, followed by steady training in the early years. You get clear explanations and a plan you can follow, so you're never guessing about what your trees need.

Connected

Together we create beauty and wonder. Trees embody this synergy.

Read more

A tree isn't an island. It's part of the whole landscape, along with the house, the walkways, the neighbors, and the wildlife. We design and care for trees as part of that whole, so they add shade, beauty, and value without fighting the space around them.

Why hire a Master Arborist?

Ben doesn't just understand how a tree grows and how to care for it. He sees the job it does in the landscape and what it will give back once it's grown. His goal is to get the most out of those benefits while cutting down on hazards and conflicts with sidewalks, roads, and buildings.

After a walk-through or a conversation about what you want from your trees, Ben writes the prescription to get you there.

Pre-care is budget friendly and gentle. Emergency care is expensive and invasive.
