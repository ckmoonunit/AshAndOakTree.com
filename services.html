Services

Care for every tree on your property.

Eight ways we look after the trees in Cache Valley yards, orchards, and properties. From a single pruning visit to a five-year management plan, every job gets the same standards and the same honest read. No woodwork left half-done.

01 Pruning

Pruning & Shaping

Most of what we do, and the work that pays off longest. Done right, pruning gives a tree a stronger structure, fewer disease problems, and a longer life. Done wrong, it shortens the tree's life and your view.

  • Structural pruning for young trees, building the framework that holds up for fifty years.
  • Crown cleaning for mature trees, removing dead, broken, and diseased wood.
  • Crown thinning for wind and light, selective and minimal.
  • Crown raising for clearance over walks, drives, and rooflines.
  • Fruit-tree pruning on Cache Valley apple, pear, peach, and stone-fruit varieties, timed to the season.
  • Restoration pruning on trees that have been neglected or poorly cut, over one to three winters.
What we do not do: topping, flush cuts, or lion-tailing. If your last guy did any of those, we can help you walk it back.
02 Removal

Tree Removal

A removal is permanent, so the conversation comes first. If the tree is dead, dying, structurally failing, or genuinely in the wrong spot, it comes down. If a removal will not actually solve your problem, we will say so.

  • Full takedowns, sectional rigging when access is tight.
  • Hazard-tree assessment before any cut.
  • Cleanup and haul-off, or chip-and-leave if you want the mulch.
  • Stump grinding as a follow-up if you want the spot replantable.
Insurance and credentials available on request. Always ask before you hire anyone.
03 Diagnosis

Health & Diagnosis

A walk-through, hands on the bark, eyes on the canopy. We check what is actually wrong before recommending what to do about it. Plenty of trees that look sick are just thirsty. Plenty that look fine are not.

  • Diagnosis for yellowing, dieback, suspicious bark, oozing, mushrooms at the base.
  • Borer and pest identification, including emerald ash borer awareness for ash trees.
  • Fire-blight assessment on pear and apple varieties.
  • Soil and watering review where it looks like a root or moisture issue.
  • A written plan you can act on, with a budget if treatment makes sense.
04 Storm Response

Storm Damage

Wind, ice, heavy snow. The valley gets all three. Storm work is where we move fastest, because what is leaning today may not be standing tomorrow.

  • Same-day response, priority for safety hazards.
  • Limb removal, hanger takedowns, broken-leader repair.
  • Salvage pruning where the tree can recover, removal where it cannot.
  • Cleanup, haul-off, and insurance documentation if you need it for a claim.
Call insurance before you call anyone with a chainsaw. That order matters.
05 Stump Grinding

Stump Grinding

Get the stump down below grade so you can replant, lay sod, or stop tripping over it.

  • Standard grind to four to six inches below grade.
  • Deeper grind on request if you are replanting in the same spot.
  • Chips left on site if you want mulch, hauled off if you do not.
  • Combined pricing if we are already removing the tree.
06 Planting

Planting & Selection

The right tree in the right spot saves you a decade of trouble. Species selection is half the job. We help you pick what will thrive in Cache Valley soils, water, sun, and winters.

  • Site walk to assess sun, drainage, soil, and proximity to utilities and structures.
  • Species recommendations matched to the spot, not the catalog.
  • Sourcing from local nurseries with good stock.
  • Proper planting depth, mulch ring, and first-year watering plan.
  • One-year check-in to catch problems early.
Trees we push back on: Bradford pear (brittle), willow near sewer lines (you know why), and most fast-growing shade trees that get sold as miracles. We can talk about why.
07 Cabling & Bracing

Cabling & Bracing

Not every structurally weak tree needs to come down. For mature trees with co-dominant stems, included bark, or stress cracks, cabling and bracing can buy the tree another twenty years and keep it from coming through your roof in a windstorm.

  • Tree assessment to determine whether structural support is the right call.
  • Steel cable installation between major limbs to reduce stress under load.
  • Threaded bracing rods for splits and weak unions.
  • Installation to ISA A300 standards, the industry baseline.
  • Annual or biannual inspection so the system keeps working as the tree grows.
When cabling is the wrong answer, we will say so. Some trees have failed past the point where hardware will hold.
08 Design

Landscape Design

Creating landscapes for new residential builds and commercial projects, from bare lot to finished planting plan. Designing a landscape is the work Ben loves most. The right trees in the right places with a watering plan, a pruning schedule, and a long-term budget, all written down on day one.

  • Landscape design for new residential homes, additions, and remodels.
  • Commercial site landscape design for retail, office, and mixed-use projects.
  • Planting plans matched to soil, sun, drainage, and Cache Valley climate.
  • Tree-management schedules for HOAs and commercial properties.
  • Long-term care budgets so the maintenance line item is honest from day one.
  • Species inventories and replacement schedules for aging landscapes.
  • Municipal landscape design, including planting plans and long-term care schedules for street and park trees.
Municipal experience Ben has done landscape and tree-management planning for American Fork City, designing planting plans and long-term care schedules for municipal trees. References available on request.
