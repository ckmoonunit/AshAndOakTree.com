Tree Care

Care for every tree on your property.

Services and maintenance plans for young to mature trees.

Specialized

The core arborist work

01 Pruning

Pruning and Shaping

The work that pays off longest. Done right, pruning means a stronger tree, fewer problems, and a longer life.

  • Structural pruning for young trees.
  • Crown cleaning, thinning, and raising for mature trees.
  • Fruit-tree pruning, timed to the season.
  • Restoration for trees that were topped or neglected.
No topping, no flush cuts, no lion-tailing. If your last guy did that, we can walk it back.
02 Diagnosis

Health and Diagnosis

We find what is actually wrong before recommending a fix.

  • Thirsty or overwatered, the most common and most missed problem.
  • Yellowing, dieback, oozing, mushrooms at the base.
  • Pest and borer ID, including emerald ash borer.
  • Fire-blight checks on pear and apple.
  • A written plan, with a budget if treatment makes sense.
03 Planting

Planting and Selection

The right tree in the right spot saves you a decade of trouble. Species selection is half the job.

  • Site walk for sun, drainage, soil, and clearances.
  • Species matched to the spot, not the catalog.
  • Proper depth, mulch ring, and first-year watering.
  • One-year check-in.
Trees we push back on: Bradford pear, willow near sewer lines, and most fast-growing shade trees sold as miracles.

Additional Services

04 Removal

Tree Removal

A removal is permanent, so the conversation comes first. If a removal will not fix your problem, we say so.

  • Full takedowns, sectional rigging for tight access.
  • Hazard assessment before any cut.
  • Cleanup and haul-off, or chip-and-leave for mulch.
  • Stump grinding as a follow-up.
05 Cabling and Bracing

Cabling and Bracing

Not every weak tree needs to come down. For mature trees with bad unions or stress cracks, hardware can buy another twenty years.

  • Assessment to see if support is the right call.
  • Steel cables between major limbs, rods for splits.
  • Installed to ISA A300 standards.
  • Inspected over time as the tree grows.
06 Stump Grinding

Stump Grinding

Stump down below grade so you can replant, lay sod, or stop tripping over it.

  • Standard grind four to six inches below grade.
  • Deeper on request for replanting.
  • Chips left for mulch or hauled off.
  • Combined pricing with a removal.
